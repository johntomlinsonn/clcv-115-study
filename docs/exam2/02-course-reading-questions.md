## Week 6 (T) 29 September
**Myths of Fertility: Demeter and Related Myths**  
**Powell 7th -9th edition: Chapter 10**  
- Find Eleusis on a map. What important sanctuary was located at Eleusis?
- What specific kinds of fertility do Artemis, Aphrodite and Hera govern?
- Who is Demeter’s daughter? What is another name for her?
- What is a wrath (menis) story? Give one example from this chapter. Give one example from a previous chapter.
- What causes Demeter’s anger, in the Homeric Hymn to Demeter? What does she do to humans out of grief for her daughter?
- What does Zeus instruct Hermes to do? What prevents him from fulfilling his mission?
- How does Zeus ultimately resolve the conflict? What does Demeter do in response?
- What is an allegory? How can the Homeric Hymn to Demeter be understood as an allegory?
- How is Persephone’s experience like that of Greek girls (parthenoi)?
- Who are Isis and Osiris? Identify three points of similarity between their story and that of Demeter and her daughter.
- Who is Adonis? What is his relationship to Aphrodite? 
- Who is Dumuzi? What is his relationship to Inanna? 
- Who is Attis? What is his relationship to Cybele? 
- Identify two points of similarity among the stories of Persephone, Osiris, Adonis and Dumuzi.
- How is Demeter in Greek literature different from Demeter in Greek religion?
- What form does Demeter take when she visits Eleusis? How is she received by the daughters of Celeus? By Metaneira, his wife?
- What task is assigned to her? How does she attempt to fulfill this task? 
- What goes wrong? What is her response?
- What famous religious cult is founded at the end of the Homeric Hymn to Demeter? Describe briefly what initiates experienced.
- What is the purpose of these rites?

- Questions
- Find Thebes on a map.

- Who were his male followers and what visuals signs distinguish them in art?
- What animals are associated with Dionysus? What plants are associated with him?
- Draw a family tree illustrating the relationships among the following: Zeus & Hera; Cadmus & Harmonia; Semele, Autonoe; Agave & Echion; Pentheus; Athamas & Ino; Dionysus & Ariadne.

- Give five examples of stories of resistance to Dionysus.

- Who wrote Bacchae and when did the author live?

- Why does Dionysus come the Thebes before other Greek cities?
- Who opposes Dionysus? Why? What does this person do to the Theban women who worship Dionysus?

- What important public events were held in Dionysus’ honor at Athens?

- What is the common theme of the stories of the loaves of bread and the “prickly herb”?
