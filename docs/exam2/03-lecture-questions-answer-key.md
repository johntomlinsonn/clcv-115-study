## Exam tips from the review lecture (show on the Exam-2 home page)
1. Study from **course reading questions** — most test questions are based on these; everything tested is on the checklist; a few come straight from lecture slides.
2. **Pronounce / listen to / write** the names (pronunciations are in Powell's index). Read answer options carefully (Amazon / Actaeon / Aeneas / Antiope look alike).
3. Bring **photo ID**.
4. If distractions are a problem, book a smaller CBTF facility at a less popular time.
5. Form a **study group**.
6. **Read the primary sources in Powell** — they are tested.
7. **Save unsure questions** and come back (PrairieTest allows it) — but watch time.
8. **Eat and sleep well**.
9. Anxious? "I can be anxious later. Right now I'm focusing on the exam."
10. **Sign up** at cbtf.illinois.edu and **take the exam by Sunday (Nov 1)**.
