## Exam facts
- **Window:** Thu **Oct 29** – Sun **Nov 1, 2026**, at the CBTF (book on PrairieTest via cbtf.illinois.edu/students; bring i-card).
- **Format:** 75 multiple-choice (a–d), 50 minutes (≈ 40 s per question). Randomized from a larger pool.
- **Content:** Powell *Classical Myth* ch. **10–14, 16–18 (not 15)** + lectures; **emphasis weeks 6–9**. All questions come from items on the checklist.
- **"Most test questions are based on the course reading questions"**; a few come straight from lecture slides. Read the primary sources in Powell — they're tested.
- **Powell's index** is available in the exam as a PDF.
- Only the **13 study-guide images** are tested (iconography only).
